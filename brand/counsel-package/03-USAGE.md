# "Verified by NPC" mark, usage notes

Plain rules for using the mark. These apply once the mark is approved for
public use. Until then, the mark stays internal.

## The first rule

The mark only appears directly next to a signed, verifiable NPC verdict.
That means: the verdict carries a valid NPC signature checked against our
published keys, and it points to an attestation ID or verification page
anyone can check. The mark is a promise that the thing beside it can be
verified. It is never decoration, never a trust garnish, and never placed
on an unknown or unverified claim. It never implies endorsement beyond what
the verdict itself states, including its scope and its time bounds.

## Clear space

Give the mark room. Clear space on all sides equals the width of the check
at its vertex (the thickest point). Nothing enters that space: no type, no
rules, no edges, no other marks.

## Minimum size

- Primary mark: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print, and up.
- Small-size variant: 16 to 32 px on screen, 5 mm in print.
- Never use any variant below 16 px. If it does not fit, use the words
  "Verified by NPC" in type instead, or drop the mark.
- Below 24 px, always switch to the small-size variant. It is cut heavier
  so the check stays legible at favicon and inline sizes.

## Color

- Default: NPC Orange, `#FF6A00`, on light or dark grounds.
- Mono black, `#181818`: one-color printing and single-color light-ground use.
- Mono white, `#FFFFFF`: one-color use on dark grounds.
- No other colors. Never recolor the mark to match a partner, a theme, or
  a season.

## What the mark must never be combined with

- Any other checkmark or verification badge, especially a blue checkmark.
- Any partner, platform, or co-brand logo locked up as one unit with the mark.
- Busy photography or patterned backgrounds behind the mark.
- Gradients, shadows, outlines, or glows applied to the mark.
- A container shape. The check stands alone. Never put it inside a circle,
  square, shield, or badge.

## What the mark must never be changed into

- Do not rotate it, stretch it, or redraw its proportions.
- Do not add type inside it or merge it with letterforms.
- Do not animate it in ways that change its shape. A simple fade or scale
  on appearance is fine.
- Do not use it as a bullet, a list marker, or a generic "done" icon. It
  means a verified verdict, nothing else.

## Approval

Every new public use of the mark gets Bayo's tap. Counsel clearance covers
the mark's public use as a program, not each placement, but any placement
that feels borderline goes back to Bayo first.
