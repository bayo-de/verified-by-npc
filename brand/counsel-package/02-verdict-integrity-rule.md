# "Verified by NPC" mark: verdict-integrity rule

Draft of record for counsel review. Date: 2026-10-06.

## What the mark signifies

The mark means one thing: the item beside it carries a signed, verifiable
NPC verdict. That means all of the following are true:

1. NPC has issued a verdict on the item.
2. The verdict carries a valid NPC signature that can be checked against
   NPC's published verification keys.
3. The verdict points to an attestation ID or verification page that anyone
   can check independently.

The mark is a promise that the thing beside it can be verified. Nothing
more, nothing less.

## What the mark never signifies

- Endorsement beyond what the verdict itself states, including its stated
  scope and time bounds.
- A claim about anything the verdict does not cover. If the verdict
  verifies a product's authenticity, the mark does not vouch for the
  seller, the listing, or the transaction around it.
- Approval, certification, or partnership by NPC as an organization,
  except to the extent the verdict text says so.
- Perpetual validity. Verdicts have time bounds. The mark travels with the
  verdict and does not outlive it.

## What the mark is never used for

- Decoration, trust garnish, or visual polish.
- Marking unknown, unverified, or unverifiable claims.
- As a bullet, a list marker, or a generic "done" icon.
- Locked up with any other checkmark or verification badge, especially a
  blue checkmark, or with any partner, platform, or co-brand logo.

## Enforcement

Every new public use of the mark gets Bayo Okusanya's approval. Counsel
clearance covers the mark's public use as a program, not each individual
placement. Any placement that feels borderline goes back to Bayo first,
and counsel may be consulted again if the placement raises new risk.

Until both Bayo's artwork approval and counsel clearance are in, the mark
stays internal and ships nowhere public.
